Bubblegum Tic Tac Toe

Tic Tac Toe is a classic two-player arcade game where strategy meets speed. Take turns placing your mark on a 3x3 grid, outsmart your opponent, and claim your victory in style!

How to Play

  1. Choose your symbol: Player 1 plays as X and Player 2 (or AI) plays as O.
  2. Take turns: Click any empty cell on the 3x3 grid to place your mark.
  3. Win the game: Align 3 of your symbols in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) before your opponent does.
  4. Draw: If all 9 squares are filled and no player has 3 in a row, the match ends in a draw!
